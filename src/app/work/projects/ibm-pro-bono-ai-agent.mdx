## The problem

A charity's staff were handling their inbox by hand: answering routine emails and drafting replies one by one.

## What I did

- Ran **usability testing** with the charity's staff to understand how they handled their inbox.
- Built a **Copilot-powered AI agent** that answers emails, drafts replies, and helps manage the inbox.
- Designed a **strategic AI implementation plan** covering grant writing, proposal drafting, blog content, and workflow optimization.

## Results

- The AI implementation plan **won first place**.
