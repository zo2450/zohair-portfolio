## The problem

A charity's staff were handling their inbox by hand: answering routine emails and drafting replies one by one.

## What I did

I ran **usability testing** with the staff to see how they actually handled their inbox, then built a **Copilot-powered agent** that answers emails, drafts replies, and helps manage the inbox. Alongside it, I designed an AI implementation plan covering grant writing, proposal drafting, blog content, and workflow optimization.
