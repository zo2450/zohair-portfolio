## The problem

Kids aged 8 to 12 love games, but few get a hands-on way to learn how they're made, especially in a real programming language.

## What I did

I co-founded **GameCraft Camp** and led it from the first cohort. Students build a 2D game in **C++** over hands-on sessions. I secured funding from **Canada Service Corps**, built partnerships with **Achēv** and **Brampton Library** to reach more students, and ran the early marketing with no paid budget.
