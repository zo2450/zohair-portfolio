## The problem

Kids aged 8 to 12 love games, but few get a hands-on way to learn how they're made, especially in a real programming language.

## What I did

- Co-founded **GameCraft Camp** and led it from the first cohort.
- Designed hands-on sessions where students build a 2D game in **C++**.
- Secured funding from **Canada Service Corps** and built partnerships with **Achēv** and **Brampton Library** to reach more students.
- Ran the early marketing with no paid budget.

## Results

- More than **1,500 students** have learned game development through the camp.
