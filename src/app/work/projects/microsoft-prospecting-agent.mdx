## The problem

Before a prospecting push, sellers and partners spent around **15 days** getting ready: pulling account information together, coordinating with each other, and tracking progress by hand.

## What I did

- Led the design and launch of the **Prospecting Automation Agent**, built with Copilot Studio, Power Automate, and Power BI.
- Automated the coordination and reporting workflows around sales blitzes.
- Co-managed and automated the **Agent Envisioning Workshops** program across AI Business Solutions.

## Results

- Prep time dropped from **15 days to 2**, an 87% reduction.
- Manual effort on blitz coordination and reporting fell by **70%**.
- The workshops program contributed to **$30M+ in pipeline revenue**.
